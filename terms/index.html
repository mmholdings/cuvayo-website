Terms & purchases

Updated November 7, 2026

Cuvayo is a podcast creation tool. This page explains the product and planned purchase options. The iPhone app’s license is governed by Apple’s standard End User License Agreement unless a separate applicable agreement is supplied.

Your content and AI assistance

You record and choose your own answers. AI hosts are fictional characters, and AI scripts, summaries, and suggestions can contain mistakes. Review them before recording, exporting, or sharing. Do not use Cuvayo to impersonate others, invent endorsements, violate someone’s rights, or upload material you do not have permission to use.

Your own recorded content remains yours. Rights in third-party music, images, voices, and generated output are subject to applicable law, your permissions, and relevant provider terms. Importing music does not give you a license to publish it.

Free creation

You can record, edit, and export creations made entirely with free tools and device voices. On-device generative AI depends on supported hardware, software, language, settings, and model availability. Template interviews are the fallback when it is unavailable.

The Pro preview

The planned preview lasts three days and includes 3 generated voice minutes, up to 4,500 UTF-8 voice bytes, 10 cloud writing actions, and 1 summary. It is available once per verified App Store app account. There is no card requirement or automatic charge when it ends.

Creations using preview Pro features can be listened to and edited, but require a paid Pro subscription to unlock export. A voice top-up alone does not unlock a preview creation.

Subscriptions and top-ups

The proposed US launch price is $9.99 per month for Pro, including 60 generated host-voice minutes, up to 90,000 UTF-8 voice bytes, 300 cloud writing actions, and 20 summaries per paid renewal. The proposed top-up is $4.99 for 30 generated voice minutes and up to 45,000 UTF-8 voice bytes. Prices, currency, availability, and final terms are shown in the App Store purchase sheet before purchase.

Paid subscriptions begin immediately and renew monthly until canceled in App Store settings. Monthly allowances do not roll over. Purchased top-up credits do not expire and remain usable after the subscription ends. Newly generated audio, including previews and regenerated lines, counts toward voice usage; your own recording time and cached replays do not. Both voice-time and text limits apply.

Apple handles billing, cancellation, and refund eligibility. Use Restore purchases in the app if needed. Deleting local data or removing the app does not cancel billing. Product availability is subject to service capacity and provider availability.

Availability and sharing

The app is currently in testing. The website’s early-access form is a launch-update list, not a payment or a guarantee of immediate access. Cuvayo exports audio and supporting text files; it does not automatically publish your show to podcast or social platforms.
