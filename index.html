What counts toward voice minutes and writing usage?

Voice minutes count newly generated AI host audio, including previews and regenerated lines. Your own recording time is free; replaying cached audio does not use more minutes. Voice allowances also have text limits: up to 90,000 UTF-8 bytes for Pro monthly, 45,000 per top-up, and 4,500 for the preview. The first applicable limit reached stops new cloud voice generation.

Writing actions include outlines, answer scripts, follow-ups, rewrites, brand scripts, and clip suggestions. Monthly allowances reset at renewal and do not roll over. Purchased top-ups remain usable after Pro ends but do not unlock exports of trial creations. Final prices and availability will be shown in the iPhone app.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
hit record.

Do I need a guest, co-host, or recording setup?

You can start on your iPhone with a topic and your own voice. Choose fictional AI hosts to guide the conversation, or use the solo format. A quiet place and headphones can help your recording sound better.

Is this my voice, or an AI version of me?

You record your own answers. The hosts are AI characters. Suggested scripts are guides you can edit, not replacements for what you actually said. You review and accept your takes.

Can I really export without subscribing?

Yes. Episodes created entirely with free tools and device voices can be exported without a paid plan. If you choose Pro features during the preview, those creations stay available to listen to and edit, but require a paid Pro subscription to unlock export.

What if my iPhone can't run on-device AI?

Free template interviews and device voices remain available. On-device generative AI requires a supported device, iOS version, language, and downloaded Apple model. Pro cloud features are a separate option when available.

Does Cuvayo publish to podcast or social platforms?

Cuvayo exports episode audio and audio clips for you to share or upload. It also supports transcript and subtitle exports. Automatic publishing and video-clip generation are not currently included.

What happens to my recordings and notes?

Projects and recordings are kept on your device. Apple speech recognition may process audio on Apple servers when on-device recognition is unavailable. Optional cloud features send relevant notes, transcripts, or host text to the selected providers with your permission. Read the privacy policy.
