Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Applies to the Cuvayo website and iPhone app

Cuvayo helps you create podcasts with your own recorded answers and fictional AI hosts. This page explains what stays on your device, what optional cloud features process, and how to contact us privately.

The short version: Your projects and recordings are stored on your device. Optional cloud features process relevant text with the providers described below. Apple speech recognition may process audio on Apple’s servers when on-device recognition is unavailable. We do not sell personal information or run advertising trackers on this website.

1. Who operates Cuvayo

Cuvayo is operated by Matthew Mordecai. Use the private privacy-request form for questions, access requests, corrections, or deletion requests. Do not post private information in a public GitHub issue.

2. Information on your device

The app stores show settings, episode details, imported reference text, scripts, recordings, transcripts, branding, and exported media in its local app storage. Device or iCloud backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Cuvayo does not automatically publish your episodes.

On-device AI, when supported and available, generates text on your device. Template interviews and Apple’s built-in device voices remain alternatives. Microphone and speech-recognition permissions are requested when needed.

3. Optional cloud AI and transcription

Cloud writing and voice features require your permission. Depending on the action you choose, relevant episode details, notes, reference text, your optional name and bio, offer details, answer transcripts, or host lines may be processed through Cuvayo’s backend and the following providers.

Earlier test versions may route cloud text and host-voice requests through Rork Toolkit and Vercel AI Gateway. The app’s data-sharing information describes the providers used by that version. Provider processing and retention are also governed by each provider’s terms and settings; we do not promise zero retention across third-party services.

Cuvayo’s direct cloud-writing integration sends text transcripts rather than your raw recorded answers. This does not change Apple speech recognition’s possible server processing. Avoid including passwords, API keys, payment-card details, or information you are not authorized to share in episode notes or scripts.

4. Purchases and usage

Apple handles in-app payments. We receive verified transaction identifiers and subscription status rather than your full payment-card details. Cuvayo’s backend keeps a pseudonymous App Store app-account reference, preview eligibility, usage totals, credit grants, and export permissions to deliver the plan you purchased and limit abuse.

The three-day Pro preview is a no-card preview, not an automatically renewing subscription. A paid subscription begins only after an App Store purchase. Deleting local app data does not cancel a subscription, erase purchased credit balances, or restart a preview.

5. Website forms and browser storage

If you join the launch list, we store the email address you provide, your opt-in, and the submission time to send Cuvayo launch updates. A support or privacy request also includes the message you submit so we can handle your request. Sending a support request does not add you to the launch list.

Our forms use a hidden anti-spam field and short-lived rate limits. This website does not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics, or cross-site tracking pixels. It can store a signup-management token in your browser so you can remove the signup associated with that browser. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request metadata, and security logs to serve and protect their services.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local projects: remain until you delete them, remove app data, or remove relevant device backups.
  • Temporary cloud results: generated text and host audio may be cached on Cuvayo’s server for about an hour to complete requests and prevent duplicate charges. Backup and provider retention may differ.
  • Usage and purchase records: may be retained as needed to maintain balances, honor purchases, prevent repeated previews or fraud, handle refunds, and meet legal or accounting obligations.
  • Website forms: are removed from the active form database after no more than 12 months, or sooner when an applicable deletion request is handled. Backups may take longer to age out.

You can turn off Pro cloud processing in Settings. That stops new cloud AI requests; it does not retract information already processed. You can delete local projects and recordings in the app. Use the private form for server-side requests; we may need to verify your control of the relevant data or purchase before acting.

Remove this browser’s launch-list signup

If this browser saved your signup token, this removes that signup directly. If you changed browsers or cleared browser storage, use the private form instead.

7. How information is shared

We share information with the providers needed to perform the features you request, with service providers operating the product, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect users and the service. Information may be processed in countries different from where you live. We do not sell your personal information or use the website to target advertisements.

8. Your choices and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other handling of personal information. Contact us privately to make a request or withdraw a launch-update opt-in. Some records may need to be retained for purchase fulfillment, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. You can also contact your local data-protection authority where that right applies.

9. Children

Cuvayo is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us through the private form so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Security and changes

We use HTTPS for website and backend requests and restrict backend access. No service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as Cuvayo develops; this page will show the effective date of the current policy.
